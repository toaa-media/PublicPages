Privacy Policy

Application: RigMaster — Rigging Sling Tension Calculator & Lift Planning System • Package: com.vn.perfectworld.rigmaster • Effective Date: September 18, 2026
Offline-First & Privacy Guarantee

RigMaster is engineered with strict privacy-by-design principles. We maintain a Zero Unauthorized Project Telemetry architecture: your rigging calculations, lift plans, equipment serials, inspection records, and lifting site data are stored strictly on your local device and are never harvested, sold, or shared.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Perfect World Studio ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you install, access, and use the RigMaster mobile application ("App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the absolute confidentiality of your professional engineering and industrial calculation data.

2. Zero Project Telemetry & Data Collection Policy

RigMaster does not collect or transmit your engineering or calculation data to external servers. Specifically:

  • No Account Registration Required: All computational capabilities, equipment databases, and lift plan generators operate without requiring registration, email login, or user profiles.
  • Zero Project Telemetry: All sling tension calculations, center of gravity (COG) calculations, equipment catalogs, crane load configurations, inspection defect logs, and digital approval signatures remain stored entirely on your local device via encrypted on-device storage.
  • No Advertising or Tracking SDKs: RigMaster contains no third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or behavioral analytics trackers.

3. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

To provide specialized industrial rigging tools, RigMaster may request access to specific device hardware features via standard operating system prompts. Each permission is used strictly locally:

Device Permissions and Local Data Handling
Permission Purpose Data Handling & Retention
Camera Scanning equipment QR/Barcode tags and capturing defect photos during equipment inspection. Photos and barcode inputs are stored locally in the application database. Zero image data is transmitted to cloud servers.
Motion Sensors (Accelerometer / Gyroscope) Powers the digital clinometer and physical sling angle measurement tool. Real-time sensor streams are calculated in volatile RAM and immediately discarded. Never recorded or transmitted.
Biometrics (Face ID / Fingerprint) Secures dual-authorization sign-offs on Critical Lift Plans (4-Eyes Approval). Authentication is handled strictly by your device's native Secure Enclave / KeyStore. No biometric signatures or sensor readings are accessible to the App.
Storage / File System Exporting PDF Lift Plan certificates and saving encrypted backup files (.rmbak). Accessed via standard system Storage Access Framework / File Pickers. Files are saved to your designated folder under your direct control.

4. Technical Crash Diagnostics & Stability Reporting

To identify software anomalies and maintain mission-critical stability, RigMaster integrates Firebase Crashlytics (operated by Google LLC).

  • Sanitized Diagnostics: Crash reports transmit technical error stack traces, operating system versions, and pseudonymous device identifiers solely for debugging.
  • PII Scrubbing: Automated redaction scrubbers actively strip authorization tokens, user passwords, project paths, and personal identifiers before transmission.
  • Third-Party Policy: Diagnostic data handling complies with Google Privacy Policy.
  • Development Deactivation: Diagnostics collection is deactivated in non-release builds and respects system-wide analytics opt-out settings.

5. Data Security & Cryptography

We implement industry-standard cryptographic mechanisms to protect local application assets:

  • Encrypted Backups: Offline database backups (.rmbak) are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with PBKDF2 key derivation (100,000 iterations). Backups cannot be decrypted without the user-defined passphrase.
  • Cryptographic Pre-Hashing: Lift Plan PDF certificates contain SHA-256 normalized manifest digests embedded in verification QR codes for tamper-evident verification.

6. Data Retention & User Control

Because your data is stored locally on your device, you maintain complete ownership and control:

  • You can export, backup, or delete individual lift plans, equipment records, and templates at any time.
  • Uninstalling the RigMaster application or clearing application data in your device settings permanently deletes all local records and stored files.

7. Children's Privacy

RigMaster is an industrial engineering application intended for qualified professionals and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
