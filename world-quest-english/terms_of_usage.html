WordQuest is an offline interactive educational application crafted to assist learners in developing foundational English skills (aligned roughly with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages, CEFR levels A1 Breakthrough through A2 Waystage).

While WordQuest incorporates structured spaced repetition, contextual grammar challenges, listening comprehension, and speaking modules, it is intended strictly as a supplementary self-study learning tool. WordQuest does not grant formal academic credits, degrees, or standardized examination certifications (such as IELTS, TOEFL, or TOEIC).

Local Data Sovereignty & Backup Responsibility

Because WordQuest does not maintain centralized servers or remote user accounts, all study progress, statistics, vocabulary notebooks, and cosmetic unlocks exist exclusively within your device's local ObjectBox database:

  • User Responsibility for Backups: If you reset your device, lose your phone, or delete the Application without exporting an encrypted backup (.wqbak), your progress cannot be restored by WordQuest administrators.
  • Cryptographic Passphrase Protection: Backups are encrypted client-side using Argon2id key derivation and AES-256-GCM. We do not know, store, or recover passphrases. If you forget the passphrase you assigned to an encrypted backup file, the data is mathematically unrecoverable.
