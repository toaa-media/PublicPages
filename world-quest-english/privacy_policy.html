Overview & Core Privacy Pledge

Welcome to WordQuest: English Adventure ("WordQuest", "we", "our", or "the Application"). WordQuest is an immersive, offline-first educational adventure application engineered to empower English language learners (CEFR A1–A2) through contextual quests, dialogue challenges, grammar battles, and pronunciation exercises.

Unlike conventional language learning applications that monetize user attention, harvest learning telemetry, or mandate continuous internet connectivity, WordQuest is engineered around a zero-trust, local-only privacy invariant. We believe your learning records, voice recordings, and personal study progression are intimate personal information that should never leave your physical smartphone or tablet.

🛡️ Our Core Privacy Guarantee

WordQuest does not collect, transmit, profile, share, monetize, or sell your personal data. 100% of curriculum content, gameplay progress, and audio self-evaluations reside exclusively on your local device storage.

2

Zero-Network Architecture Guarantee

To substantiate our commitment to total privacy, WordQuest is structured with a physically sandboxed, offline-first design:

  • Absence of Network Permissions: In the production Android build manifest (AndroidManifest.xml), the standard android.permission.INTERNET and android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE declarations are deliberately and entirely excluded. The application runtime is physically incapable of transmitting any packet over Wi-Fi or cellular networks.
  • Self-Contained Content: The complete curriculum—including four themed adventure worlds, thousands of quest dialogues, native pre-recorded pronunciation audio files, syntax rules, and assessment rubrics—ships bundled within the installed application package.
  • Guaranteed Airplane Mode Operation: WordQuest functions with full fidelity in airplane mode or in environments with zero cellular coverage.

On-Device Local Storage & Data Models

Any data generated while playing WordQuest is stored strictly inside the private, sandboxed application documents directory on your local device. This data is managed through an embedded, high-performance NoSQL local engine (ObjectBox) and secured preferences:

  • Player Profile & Avatar: An optional display moniker (e.g., "Ranger", "Scholar") and cosmetic wardrobe selections chosen by the user. This moniker is purely decorative and is never verified or shared.
  • Learning Progress & Spaced Repetition (SRS): Mastery levels, quest completion flags, review error notebooks, active streak counters, and spaced repetition intervals calculated locally via an SM-2/FSRS algorithmic curve.
  • Game State & Inventory: In-game gold coins earned exclusively through completing educational quests, badges, inventory items, and unlocked chapter nodes.
  • Application Settings: User preferences such as theme mode (Dark/Light), sound effect volume, background music toggles, haptic feedback toggles, and localized interface language.
💾 Operating System Sandboxing

Your local ObjectBox database files reside strictly within the operating system's protected application sandbox. Other applications installed on your device cannot access, read, or tamper with WordQuest's data.

Microphone & Speaking Studio Audio Hygiene

WordQuest includes a dedicated Speaking Studio feature allowing learners to practice English pronunciation by recording their speech and comparing their waveform and playback against pre-recorded native audio references.

We enforce strict technical constraints around audio handling:

  • On-Demand Recording Only: The microphone is activated only while you explicitly press and hold or tap the recording button within a Speaking Practice quest. It is never activated in the background or during other gameplay sections.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Audio recordings are stored temporarily or persistently (per user preference) strictly within the application's local sandbox. Audio data is never transmitted to cloud speech-to-text APIs or external AI evaluation engines.
  • User-Controlled Deletion: You may delete any recorded audio sample at any time directly within the Speaking Studio interface or via the Data & Privacy Management screen.

Device Permissions & Technical Rationale

WordQuest requests only the minimum runtime permissions strictly required to execute core offline educational features:

Platform Permission Category Technical Purpose & Privacy Protection
RECORD_AUDIO Microphone Required for the Speaking Studio pronunciation self-assessment module. Recorded audio remains 100% on-device and is never sent over any network.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Notifications Enables local study reminders and streak preservation alerts on Android 13+ (API 33+). Delivered purely via local device alarms without remote push notification servers.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Alarms Allows Android system alarms to trigger spaced-repetition study reminders at the precise hour selected by the user.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED System Boot Allows WordQuest to reschedule your local study alarms if your device is restarted or power-cycled.

Encrypted Portable Backups (.wqbak)

Because WordQuest rejects central cloud accounts, we provide an on-device data backup and migration utility:

  • Client-Side Encryption Pipeline: Backups are exported as a single encrypted file (.wqbak) utilizing industry-standard AES-256-GCM authenticated encryption.
  • Argon2id Key Derivation: Your user-selected passphrase is salted with a 16-byte cryptographically secure random salt and derived using Argon2id (Memory: 64MB, Iterations: 3, Parallelism: 2) to resist brute-force attacks.
  • Zero Knowledge: We do not store or transmit your passphrase. If you forget your backup passphrase, the backup cannot be decrypted.

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

WordQuest is an educational tool suitable for learners of all ages, including children, students, and families. Because WordQuest does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information (PII), we fully comply with:

  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act): We collect zero personal data from children under 13 years of age.
  • GDPR-K (European General Data Protection Regulation for Children): We process zero cloud data from children under 16 years of age in the EU/EEA.
  • Google Play Families Policy & Apple Kids Category Standards: The application contains zero behavioral advertisements, zero commercial tracking, and zero in-app purchases.

Your Rights & Data Sovereignty (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Under international privacy regulations (such as the European Union GDPR and California Consumer Privacy Act / CPRA), users have specific rights regarding their data. Because WordQuest implements 100% on-device data sovereignty, you can exercise these rights directly on your own device at any second:

  • Right to Access & Portability: You can export a full, unencrypted JSON manifest or an encrypted .wqbak archive of all your learning records directly from Settings → Data Management → Export Backup.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can permanently wipe all study logs, vocabulary progress, audio recordings, and profiles by tapping "Reset All Data" in the settings menu or by clearing the application storage via your device settings.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: WordQuest provides the exact same full, ad-free educational experience to all users worldwide without discrimination.
11

Data Retention & Permanent Deletion

All learning progress, settings, and recorded voice samples persist on your local device for as long as you choose to keep WordQuest installed. We maintain zero remote backup servers or copies.

To permanently and irrevocably erase all data:

  1. Open WordQuest and navigate to Profile → Settings → Data Management.
  2. Tap "Reset All Data" and confirm the warning prompt. All local ObjectBox tables and audio files will be deleted immediately.
  3. Alternatively, uninstalling WordQuest via your operating system (iOS or Android) triggers an immediate, unrecoverable purge of the application sandbox.
