Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Baby Profile Data: Name, birth date, gender, and metrics (weight, height, head circumference).
  • Activity Logs: Timestamps and details of feeding (breast, bottle, solids), sleep cycles, diaper changes, and vaccinations.
  • Usage Details: Anonymized interaction statistics, device type, and operating system version.

2. How We Use Information

We use the collected information to operate, maintain, and improve the BabyTracker application. Specifically, we use your data to:

  • Display interactive daily dashboards and metrics growth charts.
  • Manage vaccination alerts and reminders.
  • Facilitate local database sync and user-driven exports (PDF/Data backups).

3. Local Storage & Cloud Sync

All baby metrics, activities, and logs are stored locally on your device by default using an embedded secure database. If you choose to enable cloud backup services, your data will be synchronized securely with our servers using end-to-end encryption.

4. Data Deletion & Control

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual logs, baby profiles, or clear all app data at any time from the app's settings. Deleting the application will also erase all local data from your device.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at publisher.perfectworld.studio@gmail.com.
