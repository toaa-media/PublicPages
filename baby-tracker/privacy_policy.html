Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Information Stored Locally

BabyTracker is a fully offline application. Any information you input is stored locally and securely on your own device. We (the publishers) do not collect, store, or have access to any of this data. The types of data stored include:

  • Baby Profile Data: Name, birth date, gender, and metrics (weight, height, head circumference).
  • Activity Logs: Timestamps and details of feeding (breast, bottle, solids), sleep cycles, diaper changes, and vaccinations.
  • System Details: General configuration preferences (e.g. language, theme mode, units) kept locally.

2. How We Use Information

All baby tracking data and metrics you input are processed locally on your device to:

  • Display interactive daily dashboards and metrics growth charts.
  • Manage vaccination alerts and reminders.
  • Facilitate user-driven data exports and backups (saved locally on your device).

3. 100% Offline & Local Storage

BabyTracker is designed as a fully offline application. All baby metrics, activities, and logs are stored exclusively on your device using an embedded secure database. We do not operate any backend servers, and we never collect, transmit, or share your data over the network.

4. Data Deletion & Permanent Loss

You have full control over your data. You can delete individual logs, baby profiles, or clear all app data at any time from the app's settings. Because your data is stored 100% locally on your device, deleting the application will permanently erase all your data and cannot be recovered.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at publisher.perfectworld.studio@gmail.com.
